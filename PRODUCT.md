# Product

## Register

Both — marketing landing page (brand) and product app (product)

## Platform

Web

## Positioning

The only B2B prospecting tool built natively for Norwegian company data. Reachr connects directly to Brønnøysundregistrene (250,000+ companies) and wraps the data in a modern CRM pipeline with AI-powered outreach — purpose-built for Norwegian SMBs, not adapted from a US enterprise playbook.

## Audience

Norwegian B2B salespeople and small sales teams (1–10 people) at SMBs. They sell services or products to other Norwegian businesses. They are not technical — they want a tool that works immediately, in Norwegian, with Norwegian data.

## Brand personality

Smart, modern, confident.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS (HubSpot-style)** — over-polished, blue-heavy, corporate. Feels like every other CRM.
- **Old enterprise software** — dense tables, no whitespace, built for IT departments not salespeople.
- **American sales tools (Salesforce, Outreach)** — feels designed for US enterprise, not Norwegian SMBs.

## References

- **native.no** — warm Nordic editorial feel, confident restraint.

## Primary CTA

Start a free trial / sign up — lowest friction, get into the product as fast as possible.

## Design principles

1. **Nordic understatement** — confidence through restraint, not volume. Let the data and results speak.
2. **Speed as the product** — every interaction feels faster than the alternative. Snap-fast transitions, precise edges.
3. **Data first** — leads and pipeline are the hero; chrome serves them. The interface disappears into the work.
4. **Locally honest** — Norwegian language, data, and business norms throughout. Not a translated American product.
5. **Progressive depth** — simple to start, powerful to scale. New users see what they need; power users find depth.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA

## Lead statuses (fixed set)

`"Ikke kontaktet"` | `"Kontaktet"` | `"Kontaktet - ikke svar"` | `"Booket møte"` | `"Avslått"` | `"Kunde"`

## Belief ladder

1. **Awareness** — "I need a better way to find Norwegian B2B leads"
2. **Consideration** — "Reachr searches Brønnøysundregistrene directly — it has every Norwegian company"
3. **Decision** — "3-day free trial, no credit card, cancel anytime"
4. **Activation** — First search → first lead added → first AI email sent
